# Wotagei -- OSINT Writeup

## Challenge

**Name:** Wotagei

**Description:** \> Please find the Instagram account of the person on
the far left in the image.

**Flag Format:** `NHNC{their_instagram_username}`

## Methodology

1.  Started by searching for the group online using Google.
2.  Found the group's YouTube channel.
3.  Browsed through multiple videos and noticed several were filmed at
    the same location shown in the challenge GIF/image.
4.  Compared the appearance of the people in the videos with the
    individuals in the challenge until they appeared to match.
5.  Opened the YouTube channel description, where the members' social
    media accounts were listed.
6.  The description identified members using color references (Yellow,
    Green, and Red).
7.  Since the person on the far left matched the **Red** member, I
    copied the Red member's Instagram username.
8.  Submitted the username in the required flag format, and it was
    accepted.

## Result

The correct flag was obtained by identifying the correct member through
the YouTube channel and using the username listed in the
channel description.
